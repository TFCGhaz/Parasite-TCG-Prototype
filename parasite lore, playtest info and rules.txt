PARASITE
Trading Card Game – Playtest Guide
Version 0.1

THE WORLD OF PARASITE

Centuries ago, something arrived on Earth.

Not an army. Not a ship. Seeds.

These organisms quietly infiltrated human civilization, disguising themselves as technology. Wires were not wires, but nerve fibres. Factories were organs. Networks were living neural pathways stretching across continents.

Humanity believed it had built a technological world.

In truth, it had built a body.

Now the organism has awakened.

The ancient intelligence within this living infrastructure is known only as The Weave. Cities are beginning to change. Machines behave strangely. Entire power grids pulse with something that feels disturbingly alive.

As the truth spreads, humanity fractures into desperate factions fighting for survival, dominance, or control.

Three powers now struggle over the future of the world.

THE FACTIONS

🕸️ THE WEAVE

The original biological intelligence.

The Weave does not conquer worlds through war. It grows through them. Slowly, patiently, it spreads through infection and adaptation until resistance becomes impossible.

To the Weave, humanity is not an enemy.

It is an unfinished stage of evolution.

Playstyle:
Slow growth that becomes unstoppable if left alone.

Goal:
Spread Infection and drive the Corruption Gauge to total collapse.

Strength:
Scaling power and mutation.

Weakness:
Slow early game.

🔧 THE REMNANTS

The last organized human survivors.

When the truth about the machines was discovered, the Remnants did something desperate: they dismantled their own technology to understand it. Now they fight using weapons built from salvaged Weave tissue and scrap metal.

They are clever, ruthless, and running out of time.

Playstyle:
Aggressive, tactical, and resourceful.

Goal:
Destroy the enemy before the world falls completely to the parasite.

Strength:
Fast combat and powerful reactions.

Weakness:
Limited long-term scaling.

☣️ THE SEVERED

Not fully human.

Not fully Weave.

The Severed are survivors of partial infection. Something interrupted the parasite before it finished integrating them. The result is something new — people who can sense and manipulate both sides.

They do not fight for humanity.

They fight for leverage.

Playstyle:
Manipulation, control, and opportunism.

Goal:
Exploit both sides and accumulate Leverage.

Strength:
Control and trickery.

Weakness:
Unpredictable and complex to play.

VICTORY CONDITIONS

Each faction wins differently.

The Weave
Win instantly if the Corruption Gauge reaches 20 OR if 10 Infection tokens exist on enemy targets.

The Remnants
Reduce the opponent's Life Pool to 0.

The Severed
Collect 15 Leverage tokens.

Each player begins with 20 Life.

TURN ORDER CHEAT SHEET

Each turn follows the same six phases.

UPKEEP

Resolve start-of-turn effects.

Gain your base resources:
Weave → Biomass
Remnants → Scrap
Severed → Flux

Weave units gain 1 Growth Token.

DRAW

Draw 1 card from your deck.

If you cannot draw, lose 2 Life.

MAIN PHASE A

You may:

Play Units
Play Action cards
Activate abilities
Position units near Locations

Units played this turn cannot attack unless they have SURGE.

COMBAT

Choose which units attack.

Opponent chooses blockers.

Combat damage happens simultaneously.

Destroyed units go to the discard pile.

Unblocked units deal damage to the opponent's Life Pool.

MAIN PHASE B

Play more cards or activate abilities.

The Weave may trigger Mutations during this phase if requirements are met.

END PHASE

Resolve end-of-turn effects.

Discard down to 7 cards.

Pass the turn.

LOCATIONS

Three Locations are placed in the centre of the board.

A location is:

Controlled
Only one player has units near it.

Contested
Both players have units near it.

Locations provide bonuses depending on control.

CORRUPTION GAUGE

The Corruption Gauge tracks the spread of The Weave.

It increases when:

Weave units survive combat
Infection tokens are placed
At the start of the Weave player's turn

It decreases when:

Weave units are destroyed
Infection is removed
Remnants perform Purge actions

If the gauge reaches 20, The Weave wins instantly.
