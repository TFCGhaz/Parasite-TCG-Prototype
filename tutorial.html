Section 01

What is PARASITE TCG?

PARASITE is a two-player strategic card game. Each player controls a faction of units, deploys them to a shared battlefield, and tries to fulfil their faction's unique win condition before the opponent fulfils theirs.

Unlike many card games, PARASITE does not have a single win condition for both sides. The Weave wins by spreading Infection and maxing out the Corruption Gauge. The Remnants win by reducing their opponent's Life to zero. This means both players are playing a fundamentally different game — even when they're sitting across the same table.

🎯 The Golden Rule
When in doubt about a rule, the action log on the right side of the screen records everything that happens. Click the "ACTION LOG" tab to open it and trace back what just occurred.
Section 02

Setting Up

  1. Open index.html in a browser. The game starts automatically.
  2. Player 1 plays The Remnants ⚡ (bottom of the screen). Player 2 plays The Weave 🕸️ (top of the screen).
  3. Both players are automatically dealt 5 cards from their shuffled deck.
  4. A pass screen covers the hand between turns — tap "I'M READY" only once the correct player is watching.
  5. Player 1 (Remnants) always goes first.
💡 Pass Screen
Because both players share one screen, the pass screen prevents the other player from peeking at your hand. Always wait until the screen is covered before passing the device.

Section 03

Reading a Card

Every card has the same layout. Here's what each part means:

PartWhat it means
🕸️ / ⚡ top-leftFaction icon — 🕸️ is The Weave, ⚡ is The Remnants.
Number top-rightCost — how many resources you must spend to play this card.
Big emoji in the middlePlaceholder art — represents the card's creature or effect.
⚔ bottom-left (units only)Attack (ATK) — damage dealt in combat.
🛡 bottom-right (units only)Defence (DEF) — damage absorbed. When DEF reaches 0, the unit is destroyed.
Text bar (small strip)Card type: UNIT or ACTION.

To read a card's full ability text, click it anywhere — a detail panel appears on the left side of the screen with the full ability description and flavour text.

💡 Units vs Actions
Unit cards are placed on the battlefield and stay there until destroyed. Action cards have a one-time effect and go straight to the discard pile after being played. Actions have a dashed border to tell them apart.
Section 04

Turn Structure

Every turn has 6 phases, shown by the dots in the header. The current phase is highlighted in gold. Press Next Phase → to advance.

01 UPKEEP
Gain your resources for this turn.

Both players gain 2 base resources (Scrap for Remnants, Biomass for Weave). Weave players also grow all their units by +1 Growth Token and the Corruption Gauge rises by 1. Remnants get a bonus +1 Scrap if Corruption is 5 or below. This phase happens automatically — just press Next Phase.

02 DRAW
Draw 1 card from your deck.

Press Next Phase to draw. If your deck is empty, you take 2 damage instead. Plan your resources carefully — running out of cards hurts.

03 MAIN A
Play cards from your hand.

Click a card in your hand to select it (it lifts up). Then press Play Card. You can play as many cards as you can afford. Units played this phase cannot attack immediately (Summoning Fatigue) unless they have the SURGE keyword.

04 COMBAT
Declare attackers and fight.

Press Declare Attack. Click one of your un-exhausted units to select it as the attacker — it will glow. Then click an enemy unit to fight it, or press Attack Player to deal damage directly to the opponent (only if they have no units, or you have PIERCING). You can attack multiple times — once per un-exhausted unit. Press Next Phase when done.

05 MAIN B
Play more cards (second chance).

Identical to Main A. Useful for playing cards after seeing how combat resolved. Weave players trigger Mutations here if growth is complete.

06 END
Discard to hand limit, pass turn.

If you have more than 7 cards, the oldest ones are discarded automatically. All ATK boosts from this turn reset. Press Next Phase to trigger the pass screen and hand off to the other player.

Section 05

How Combat Works

Combat in PARASITE is simultaneous. Both the attacker and the defender deal their ATK to each other at the same time. There is no separate "blocking" phase — the defending player simply chooses which of their units to put in the way.

Step-by-step

  1. Press Declare Attack to enter combat mode.
  2. Click one of your units. It will glow red — this is your attacker.
  3. Click an enemy unit — both deal damage simultaneously.
  4. If either unit's DEF drops to 0 or below, it is destroyed.
  5. The attacker becomes Exhausted and cannot attack again this turn.
  6. Repeat with other un-exhausted units if you wish.
⚠ Attacking is optional
You never have to attack. Sometimes the best move is to build up units and wait. Attacking a high-DEF unit with a low-ATK unit is usually a bad trade — your unit might die and deal no meaningful damage.

Direct Attacks

If your opponent has no units on the field, you can press Attack Player instead of clicking a unit. This deals your attacker's ATK directly to their Life total. Units with PIERCING can also attack the player directly regardless of whether the opponent has units.

Infection Penalty

Infected units (shown with a ☣ badge) lose 1 ATK in combat. A unit with ATK 2 that has an infection token deals only 1 damage. Keep your units clean with Purge Protocol or Combat Medic.

Section 06

Keywords Reference

SURGE

This unit can attack the same turn it is played. Normally units suffer Summoning Fatigue and must wait a turn.

PIERCING

Excess damage carries through to the opponent's Life. If a PIERCING unit has ATK 4 and the blocker has DEF 1, the opponent loses 3 Life.

HIVE

Weave keyword. This unit gains bonuses based on how many other HIVE units you control. Isolating them weakens them.

PURIFIER

When this unit enters play, remove 1 Infection token from a friendly unit. Crucial for Remnants keeping their units clean.

VOLATILE

When destroyed, deals 2 damage to a random enemy unit. The Weave sometimes destroys its own VOLATILE units intentionally.

MUTATION

Weave-only. When a unit accumulates enough Growth Tokens (usually 3), it transforms into a far more powerful version mid-game.

Section 07

The Factions

⚡ THE REMNANTS

Aggressive, efficient, and surgical. Hit hard and fast before The Weave gets established. Every unit death generates Scrap resources. Strong early game, can struggle if the game goes long.

Best strategy: apply pressure early, use Purge Protocol to stay clean, and save Last Stand for when you're low on Life.

WIN: Reduce opponent's Life to 0

🕸️ THE WEAVE

Patient, inevitable, and exponentially powerful. Start small, grow your units, spread Infection, and overwhelm the board. Weak in the early game but almost unstoppable if given time.

Best strategy: protect your Mycelian Crawlers until they Mutate, use Spore Burst carefully, and push Corruption higher whenever possible.

WIN: Push Corruption Gauge to 20
Section 08

The Corruption Gauge

The Corruption Gauge is a shared track in the top-right corner of the screen, ranging from 0 to 20. It represents how far The Weave's biological takeover has spread.

RangeEffect
0–5Low corruption. Remnants gain +1 bonus Scrap each Upkeep.
6–10Rising. Weave units gain +1 DEF. Infected units deal -1 damage.
11–15Critical. Weave Mutations require 1 fewer Growth Token.
16–19Near-collapse. Remnants' most powerful cards unlock.
20The Weave wins immediately.

The gauge rises when: a Weave unit survives combat (+1), a Weave turn begins (+1), a Spore Node is on the field (extra +1 per node), Creeping Rot is played (+1), or a Mutation completes (+2).

The gauge falls when: a Weave unit is destroyed (-1), Purge Protocol is played (-2), or Infection tokens are removed.

⚠ Remnants: Watch the gauge!
The Corruption Gauge is your clock. If you're not killing Weave units and cleaning Infections, you will lose the gauge race even if you're winning in combat. Purge Protocol is one of your most important cards.

Section 10

Tips for New Players

For Remnants Players

  • Go wide early. Militia Grunts and Salvage Runners are cheap. Flood the field while The Weave is still building.
  • Kill Crawlers before they Mutate. A Crawler with 2 growth tokens is a 1-turn timer. Prioritise them above everything else.
  • Save Purge Protocol. Creeping Rot can infect your entire field in one move. Having a Purge Protocol in hand is your insurance policy.
  • Watch the gauge. If Corruption hits 10+, The Weave's DEF bonuses make them very hard to kill. Act before that.
  • Don't overcommit. Sending a 1-ATK unit into a 3-DEF unit just trades your unit for nothing. Pick your fights.

For Weave Players

  • Protect your Crawlers. Mycelian Horror (the mutated form) is the most powerful unit in the game. Keeping Crawlers alive for 3 turns is your top priority.
  • Use Spore Node as a shield. Its 0 ATK makes it a useless attacker, but it has 4 DEF and punishes units that attack it with Infection.
  • Biomass Surge is free. Play it whenever you need the extra push — it costs 0.
  • Spore Burst is a sacrifice. It lets you trade a weak unit for 2 Infection tokens on the strongest enemy unit. Use it when you have a unit that's about to die anyway.
  • Corruption is your clock. Every turn that passes without you dying, your position improves. Stall, defend, and grow.
🎮 Playing your first game
For your very first game, don't worry about winning. Focus on understanding the turn flow: Upkeep (gain resources) → Draw (get a card) → Main A (play stuff) → Combat (attack) → Main B (play more stuff) → End (pass). After one full game you'll have an intuitive sense of the rhythm.
